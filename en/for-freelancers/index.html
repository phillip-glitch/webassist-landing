FAQ

Common Questions

Does WebAssist work with my portfolio website?+

Yes — WebAssist works with any website platform including WordPress, Webflow, Wix, Squarespace and fully custom-built portfolio sites. During onboarding we connect to your existing site.

Can I add a new case study via WhatsApp?+

Absolutely. Just describe the project, send any images and we'll add the case study to your portfolio the same day. You'll receive a confirmation when it's live.

Can I update my availability and rates?+

Yes. Send "I'm now available from March" or "Update my day rate to €800" via WhatsApp and we'll update your website immediately so clients always see accurate information.

Do I need any technical skills?+

Not at all. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use WebAssist. There's no login, no CMS, no technical knowledge required.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled with 30 days notice. There are no long-term contracts.
