FAQ

Common Questions

Can you work with my existing website?+

Yes. We work with WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Wix, Squarespace, and most custom-built websites. During onboarding we connect to your existing site — no migration needed.

How fast are updates completed?+

Most updates are completed the same day, often within a few hours. Complex changes like new landing pages may take 24–48 hours. You always receive a confirmation message when your website is live.

Do I need technical skills?+

Not at all. If you can send a WhatsApp message, you can use WebAssist. There's no login, no backend, no technical knowledge required. Just type what you want changed.

Can I cancel anytime?+

Yes. Monthly subscriptions can be cancelled with 30 days notice. There are no long-term contracts on monthly plans. Annual plans are billed upfront.

What happens if I need bigger changes?+

For major redesigns or entirely new websites, we can discuss a custom project quote. Our Business plan covers most advanced changes including new landing pages, blog posts, and full content overhauls.
