WebAssist vs Wix: The Easiest Way to Manage Your Website in 2026

Wix calls itself "the easiest website builder" — but is it really easy when you're running a business and don't have time to learn another platform? We compare Wix and WebAssist head-to-head.

Wix Is Easy to Build — But Is It Easy to Manage?

Wix has done an impressive job making it simple to create a website from scratch. Drag-and-drop design, hundreds of templates, no coding required. If you're starting a new site with a free afternoon, Wix is genuinely beginner-friendly.

But most small business owners don't need to build a website — they already have one. What they need is to manage it: update prices, swap out photos, add a new service, change opening hours. And that's where things get more complicated.

Every update to a Wix site requires you to log in on a laptop (the mobile editor is limited), find the right element, drag it around, and republish. It's a process that takes 10–30 minutes for changes that should take 30 seconds to describe in plain English.

What Makes WebAssist Different

WebAssist takes a completely different approach. Instead of giving you another dashboard to learn, it gives you a WhatsApp conversation. You describe what you need in plain language — "Can you update my price list?" or "Add a photo of our new product to the homepage" — and it gets done.

There's no platform to migrate to. WebAssist works alongside your existing website, whether it's on Wix, WordPress, Webflow, Squarespace, or any other platform. You keep what you have and gain the ability to update it effortlessly.

It's the difference between a tool that makes a task easier and a service that makes the task disappear from your to-do list entirely.

What if updating your website was as easy as sending a text?

With WebAssist, it is. Send a WhatsApp message, get your website updated the same day.

The Real Cost of Wix

Wix's pricing looks reasonable at first glance — €17–35/month for a business plan. But there's a hidden cost: your time. Every website update takes you away from running your business. Over a month, those small updates add up to hours.

And if you have a Wix site but hired someone to build it, you may find the Wix editor confusing if you didn't build it yourself. Templates and apps modify the interface significantly, and what looks simple in a tutorial video may not match your specific site structure.

WebAssist at €99/month looks more expensive on the surface — but includes unlimited small updates handled for you. For most business owners, the time savings alone justify the difference.

Wix's Vendor Lock-In Problem

One of Wix's biggest drawbacks is rarely mentioned in comparisons: you're locked in. Your website lives on Wix's servers in Wix's proprietary format. You can't export it to another platform. If Wix raises prices, changes features, or simply doesn't offer what you need in the future, you're starting over.

WebAssist has no such problem because it doesn't host your site. It works with whatever you have — and you remain in full ownership of your web presence.

When Wix Makes Sense

Choose Wix if...

  • You're building a brand-new website and have no existing one
  • You genuinely enjoy designing and maintaining your site yourself
  • Your website is very simple (a portfolio, a landing page) with infrequent updates
  • You want full visual control over every element at any time
  • Your budget is extremely tight and time is not a constraint

When WebAssist Makes Sense

Choose WebAssist if...

  • You already have a website and just need it kept up to date
  • You want to manage everything from your phone without a separate app
  • Website updates feel like a chore you put off every week
  • You want same-day turnaround without managing anyone or anything
  • You don't want to be locked into any single platform

The Bottom Line

Wix is a good tool for what it's designed to do: help non-technical people build a website. But building and managing are different jobs. If you want the simplest possible way to manage a website — make changes, keep it current, keep it working — WebAssist is the clear winner.

There is nothing simpler than sending a WhatsApp message. And that's exactly how WebAssist works.
