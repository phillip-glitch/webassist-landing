FAQ

Common Questions

Can I add gig dates via WhatsApp?+

Yes — send venue, date, time, city and ticket price via WhatsApp and we'll add it to your events page the same day with proper formatting.

Can I announce a new release on my website?+

Absolutely. Send the release name, date, streaming links and cover art via WhatsApp and we'll update your discography and create an announcement.

Can I remove past gigs automatically?+

Yes. Either send a message to remove specific dates, or we can set up automatic removal of past events to keep your calendar clean.

Does it work with Bandcamp, Spotify or Soundcloud links?+

Yes. Send any streaming links and we'll embed or link them properly on your website so fans can listen directly.

Can I manage my press kit via WhatsApp?+

Absolutely. Send new bio text, press photos or EPK updates and we'll keep your press page current for bookers and journalists.
