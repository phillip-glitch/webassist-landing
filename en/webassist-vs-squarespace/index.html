WebAssist vs Squarespace: Which Is Best for Non-Technical Business Owners in 2026?

Squarespace is one of the most beautiful website builders on the market. But beautiful design and easy updates are two very different things. Here's an honest look at which tool actually works better for busy small business owners.

Squarespace Is Gorgeous — But Is It Easy?

Squarespace has built a well-deserved reputation for stunning templates and polished design. If you've seen their ads — or tried to build a portfolio site on a Sunday afternoon — you know the appeal. Everything looks professional right out of the box.

But here's the part nobody puts in the ads: even on Squarespace, you still have to log in, navigate a CMS, find the right section, make your edit, and publish. For a business owner who just wants to change their opening hours or update a price list, that process adds up to real minutes — and real frustration — every single time.

That's the problem WebAssist was designed to solve. Instead of any dashboard at all, you send a WhatsApp message. Done.

What Is WebAssist?

WebAssist is a fully managed website update service. You communicate through WhatsApp — the app already on your phone — and a trained assistant makes the change for you, typically the same day. No logins, no design software, no learning curve.

Unlike Squarespace, WebAssist is not a website builder. It works with your existing website, whatever platform it runs on. If you already have a Squarespace site, WebAssist can manage it. If you're on a custom build, WordPress, or Wix, the same service applies.

We also work with restaurants and clinics who need fast, regular updates without the hassle.

Squarespace: The Full Picture

Before we compare, let's be fair. Squarespace is a genuinely excellent product in many situations. Here's an honest breakdown:

Squarespace Strengths

  • Industry-leading design templates — websites look professional with minimal effort
  • All-in-one: hosting, domain, SSL, and CMS included in one subscription
  • Built-in e-commerce for selling products or services
  • Good blogging tools and SEO basics built in
  • Reliable hosting — almost no downtime
  • No developer needed for initial setup

Squarespace Weaknesses

  • You still need to log in and learn the CMS for every update — no escaping it
  • Mobile editing experience is limited — some changes only work properly on desktop
  • Monthly cost starts at ~€16/month and rises quickly for business features
  • Locked into the Squarespace ecosystem — migrating away is painful
  • Limited customisation outside their template system
  • Updates still require your time and attention, even if simpler than WordPress

What if updating your site was as easy as sending a text?

With WebAssist, you send a WhatsApp message and your website is updated the same day — no logins, no CMS, no learning curve.

The Real Cost of Squarespace

Squarespace's pricing looks reasonable at first glance — around €16–35/month depending on the plan. But there's a cost that doesn't appear on the pricing page: your time.

Every time you need to update your menu, change a service price, swap out a team photo, or announce a new offer, you're spending 10–30 minutes in a CMS. For a business owner who makes these kinds of updates once or twice a week, that's easily 2–3 hours per month spent on website administration instead of serving customers.

WebAssist at €99/month removes that overhead entirely. Many business owners find the time savings alone are worth far more than the subscription cost.

When Squarespace Is the Right Choice

Choose Squarespace if...

  • You're building a portfolio, personal brand, or design-focused site
  • You enjoy managing your own website and updating it yourself
  • You need built-in e-commerce and don't want a separate solution
  • You're starting from scratch and want a beautiful result quickly
  • Your budget is tight and you're willing to do the work yourself

When WebAssist Is the Right Choice

Choose WebAssist if...

  • You're a non-technical business owner who wants updates handled, not managed
  • You want to request changes from your phone — exactly like sending a text
  • You already have a website that works fine, on any platform
  • You're tired of logging into a CMS to make simple changes
  • You want a predictable monthly cost with no surprise developer invoices

Verdict: Two Tools for Two Different People

Squarespace is an excellent website builder for people who want a beautiful site and are willing to log in and manage it themselves. If design is your priority and you enjoy the process, it's one of the best tools available.

But if you're running a restaurant, a clinic, a consultancy, or any service business where your time is better spent on customers than on a CMS — WebAssist is the smarter choice. You get same-day updates via WhatsApp, zero technical overhead, and full management included.
