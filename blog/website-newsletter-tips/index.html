How to Build an Email List From Your Website

Email is the highest-ROI marketing channel. Build a responsive list from your website.

Add an Email Signup Form

Place a signup form above the fold on your homepage. "Get tips and updates to your email." Keep it simple: just email address (and optionally name).

Offer a Freebie (Optional but Powerful)

"Subscribe for a free checklist," "Get 10% off," or "Free video guide." This significantly increases signup rate.

Use Email Marketing Software

Mailchimp (free for up to 500), ConvertKit, Klaviyo, or ActiveCampaign. These manage your list and send broadcasts.

Place Signup Everywhere

Homepage, blog posts, footer, services page, thank you page. Multiple opportunities = more signups.

Send Regularly

Weekly is ideal. Monthly minimum. No more than weekly or people unsubscribe. Quality over quantity.

Content Worth Sending

Not just sales pitches. Share tips, advice, announcements, stories. Make people want to open your emails.

Segment Your List

Send different messages to different segments (past customers vs. prospects). Better open rates and conversions.
