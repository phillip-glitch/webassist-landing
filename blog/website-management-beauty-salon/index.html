Website Management for Beauty Salons: Update Prices & Services Instantly

Beauty salons update prices, services, and staff availability constantly. Yet most salon websites are static and outdated. Learn how modern salons keep their websites current without expensive developer retainers.

Why Beauty Salon Websites Need Constant Updates

A beauty salon website isn't like a corporate site that stays the same for months. Your salon changes multiple times a week: a stylist books time off, a new service gets added, prices shift based on supply costs, seasonal promotions launch and expire. Your website needs to reflect this reality — otherwise customers book appointments at the wrong price, request services that aren't available, or book time with stylists who aren't working.

The traditional model doesn't work for salons. You hire a developer to build your site once, then you're stuck. Every price change, every new service, every staff update requires an email, a wait, and a fee. By the time the developer updates your site, you've already lost bookings because your online presence doesn't match your actual business.

Modern salon owners are taking control back. They're updating their websites themselves — instantly, without technical knowledge, sometimes literally from their phone in the salon while talking to a client.

The Five Updates Salons Make Most Frequently

If you run a salon, you probably need to update these things regularly:

1. Service Pricing

Your haircut was €40 last year. Now it's €50. This information needs to update on your website immediately — not three days from now. A customer checking your prices before booking should see accurate information. If they show up expecting €40 cuts and find out it's €50, they're frustrated and you've lost goodwill.

2. Service Menu and Descriptions

You introduce a new balayage technique. You discontinue a slow-selling service. You bundle services into a new package. These changes need to appear on your website. The more detailed your service descriptions, the better customers can decide what to book and prepare mentally for the appointment.

3. Stylist Availability and Profiles

A popular stylist books vacation. A new colorist joins the team. Someone specializes in bridal hair. These details drive booking decisions. Customers want to know who they're booking with, what that person specializes in, and when they're available. If your stylist profiles are outdated, you'll lose appointments to salons with current information.

4. Promotional Offers

Summer specials. First-time customer discounts. Referral bonuses. Product sales. These promotions are time-sensitive and high-impact. The faster you can add them to your website and social media, the faster bookings will come in. A promotion that goes live a week late is less effective than one that's live immediately.

5. Class Schedules and Workshops

Many salons offer styling classes, workshops, or training. These schedules change frequently and need to be accurate. If a customer signs up for a class that got rescheduled but the website still shows the old date, you've created a problem.

The Cost of Slow Website Updates for Salons

Consider a typical mid-size salon with 8 stylists and about 15 regular updates per month (service changes, pricing, staff info, promotions):

  • Developer cost per update: €100–€250 (minimum 30 minutes of work)
  • Updates per month: 15
  • Monthly developer cost: €1,500–€3,750
  • Annual cost: €18,000–€45,000 just for website maintenance

Plus the time cost: emailing, writing briefs, waiting for replies, reviewing changes, dealing with mistakes. That's 5–10 hours per month your staff could be spending on clients instead of website management.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I have a custom salon booking system?+

WebAssist integrates with most booking systems (Salon Pulse, Vagaro, Styleseat, etc.). Your website content updates happen separately from your booking system. We can sync availability information so your online calendar stays current.

Can I let multiple staff members update the website?+

Yes. You can assign different team members their own WhatsApp connections with specific permissions. Your manager might handle pricing changes, while stylists can submit new photos or request time off.

Will updates go live immediately or do I have to approve them?+

Most updates (prices, text, availability) go live same-day, typically within a few hours. For major changes or photo additions, you can enable a preview-first option to review before the update goes live.

What about before/after photos? How do those work?+

Send a message saying "Add these two photos to the balayage gallery" and upload the images via WhatsApp. We'll add them, optimize them for web, and display them on your site — no upload form, no resizing, no technical steps.
