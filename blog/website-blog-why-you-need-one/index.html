Do You Need a Blog on Your Business Website? (Honest Answer)

Blogging is powerful but also time-consuming. When should you actually have a blog?

Blogs Work If...

You publish consistently (at least monthly). You're in a competitive market where SEO matters. You can write decent content or hire someone. You want to establish authority. You have time or budget for content creation.

Blogs Don't Work If...

You only post sporadically (3-4 posts then nothing). You run a specialized service where customers don't search for tips. You don't have time to maintain it. An abandoned blog hurts credibility more than no blog.

What Blogs Actually Deliver

SEO traffic—you rank for long-tail keywords that drive customers. Authority—you show expertise. Email signups—blog readers subscribe. Relationship building—regular readers become clients over time. But this takes months of consistent posting to see results.

The Honest Truth

A small business blog needs 2-4 posts monthly minimum to work. That's 4-8 hours monthly. If you won't commit to that, skip blogging. Focus on other marketing instead (Google Ads, local SEO, referrals).

Alternative: Guest Posting

Write articles for industry sites instead. You get authority and backlinks without maintaining your own blog.
