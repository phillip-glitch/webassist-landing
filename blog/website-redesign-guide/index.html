When and How to Redesign Your Business Website

Does your site need a redesign? Here's how to decide and execute it right.

Signs You Need a Redesign

Your design looks outdated (3-5+ years old). Mobile experience is poor. Conversion rate is low despite good traffic. Bounce rate is high (50%+). Your design doesn't match your current brand. You're losing customers to competitors with better-looking sites.

Redesign Goals

Before starting, ask: What am I trying to improve? More conversions? Better brand perception? Mobile experience? Faster load times? Be specific. A vague redesign wastes money.

How to Redesign

Step 1: Plan

Define goals. Audit current site. Research competitor designs. Decide on new structure.

Step 2: Design

Create mockups. Get feedback. Iterate. Don't just redesign for beauty—optimize for conversion.

Step 3: Build

Develop the new site. Test thoroughly on mobile and desktop.

Step 4: Migrate

Move content. Set up 301 redirects from old URLs to new ones. This preserves SEO rankings.

Step 5: Launch

Go live. Monitor closely first 48 hours. Be ready to fix issues.

Common Mistakes

Changing all URLs without redirects (kills SEO). Redesigning without data (ignoring what actually converts). Making it less usable (form with more fields). Ignoring mobile users (50% of traffic).
