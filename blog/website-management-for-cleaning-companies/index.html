Website Management for Cleaning Companies: A Practical Guide

Running a cleaning business means pricing changes, new services, and customer testimonials flow constantly. Learn how to keep your website updated and competitive without a developer.

Why Cleaning Companies Can't Keep Their Websites Current

You're a cleaning company owner. You're managing a team of cleaners, coordinating schedules, responding to customer inquiries, and handling bookings. Your website is important — potential customers visit it every day — but it's not where your hands-on work happens.

So when you raise prices because supply costs increased, your website still shows the old rates for three weeks. When you add a new specialized service like commercial floor waxing, you forget to add it to the services list. When a customer leaves a glowing testimonial about your reliable team, it sits in an email instead of appearing on your site.

The problem: updating your website requires contacting a developer, explaining what you want changed, waiting for them to do it, and often paying €75-€150 per update. For a cleaning business doing 5-8 updates per month, that's €375-€1,200 every month just to keep your site reasonably current.

Most cleaning companies just don't bother. And the result is a website that's slowly falling behind reality, looking less competitive than it should.

What Cleaning Companies Actually Need to Update

Cleaning businesses have specific, recurring website needs:

1. Service pricing and packages

As costs change or you introduce special promotions, your pricing needs to update. A website showing outdated rates confuses potential customers and damages your reputation when they realize the prices have changed.

2. New service offerings

You add a new service: deep cleaning, carpet cleaning, window cleaning, or commercial maintenance. Getting this on your website should take hours, not weeks.

3. Customer testimonials and reviews

Happy customers provide testimonials. These are gold for conversion. Getting them on your site quickly builds social proof and credibility with new prospects.

4. Service areas and coverage zones

As you grow, you might expand into new neighborhoods or nearby cities. Your website needs to reflect your actual coverage area so customers know you serve them.

5. Seasonal promotions and special offers

Spring cleaning specials, year-end promotions, referral discounts. These need to go live quickly when you launch them and come down immediately when they expire.
