How to Add a Booking System to Your Website

Online booking increases conversions, reduces phone time, and looks professional. Learn how to add it without technical skills.

Why Booking Systems Matter

Customers prefer online booking. They can book at 2am without calling you. You get fewer calls, cleaner scheduling, and automatic confirmation emails. It looks professional and modern.

Easiest Solutions

Calendly (simplest)

Calendly is the easiest. Create a free account, set your availability, share the link. Customers book directly into your calendar. Automatic confirmations and reminders.

Acuity Scheduling

More powerful than Calendly. Handles multiple staff, custom forms, automatic invoicing. Still simple for non-technical users.

Setmore

Free for small businesses. Handles appointments, services, pricing, staff. Integrates with your website easily.

How to Integrate

Most systems provide an embed code or link. Add the link to your website navigation or a page. That's it. Customers click, book, done.

Best Practice

Place the booking link prominently. On your homepage. In the navigation. On your services page. Make it obvious—every page should have a "Book now" button.
