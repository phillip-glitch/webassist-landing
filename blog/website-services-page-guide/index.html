How to Write a Services Page That Gets More Bookings

Your services page is where most visitors go to understand what you do. Write it well—it drives bookings.

Start With Customer Problems, Not Features

Don't: "We offer plumbing services." Do: "Burst pipes? Slow drains? We fix them fast—usually same day."

List Services Clearly

Use a bulleted list or cards. For each service: name, brief description, price (if applicable), and call-to-action. Make it scannable.

Include Pricing

Hiding pricing hurts you. If price is variable, say "From €X" or "Get a quote." Transparency builds trust.

Add Small Photos or Icons

Visual breaks in text improve readability. A small icon next to each service helps scanning.

Social Proof

"500+ happy customers served" or client testimonials specific to each service build credibility.

Clear CTAs

After each service or at the bottom: "Book now," "Get a quote," or "Learn more."

Answer Common Questions

FAQs section addressing: "How long does it take?" "What's the cost?" "What's your warranty?" etc.
