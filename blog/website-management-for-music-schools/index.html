Website Management for Music Schools & Tutors

Manage lesson schedules, instructor profiles, and pricing for your music school instantly. No developer, no CMS learning curve—just WhatsApp messages that update your site.

The Problem: Outdated Music School Schedules

You're running a music school. You have multiple instructors, each with their own schedules. Students check your website to book lessons with specific teachers. But your website shows outdated information because updating it requires emailing a developer. A student sees that Monday 4 PM is available for piano lessons with Sarah. They call to book. You tell them Monday is fully booked—the website wasn't updated. They book with a competitor.

Music schools change constantly. New instructors join. Existing instructors change their availability. Prices adjust. New instruments and courses are offered. Group lessons are added or removed. Your website should reflect these changes immediately, but it doesn't because traditional website management is too slow.

What Changes at a Music School

Instructor availability and schedules

When you hire a new piano teacher, their schedule needs to go live immediately. When an instructor reduces hours or leaves, their availability must be updated instantly. Same-day changes need to be reflected before the next student checks your website.

Pricing and lesson packages

You adjust rates seasonally. You bundle lessons into packages. You offer discounts for first-time students. Your pricing page must always reflect current rates, or you'll spend time on phone calls explaining price discrepancies.

Class rosters and group lessons

You add a new beginner group piano class on Wednesday evenings. You need to announce it, show availability, and update course details. A traditional developer would need 5-7 days to make this live.

Instructor bios and credentials

When you hire a qualified teacher, their credentials and bio need to be visible—quickly. A delayed profile means missed referrals and lost students.
