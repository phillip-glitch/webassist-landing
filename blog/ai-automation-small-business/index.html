AI Automation for Small Business: Where to Start in 2026

AI can automate repetitive tasks and free your time. Here's where to start.

Website Management (WebAssist)

Instead of managing your site yourself or emailing developers, use AI. WebAssist via WhatsApp saves 5-10 hours monthly of website management.

Email Management (AI Assistants)

Tools like Zapier or Make (formerly Integromat) can automate email responses, file sorting, and data entry. Setup takes 1-2 hours, saves weeks monthly.

Customer Service (Chatbots)

AI chatbots answer common questions 24/7. Reduces support email volume by 30-40%. Examples: Intercom, Drift, or WhatsApp Business API.

Content Creation (ChatGPT, Claude)

Write email copy, social posts, blog articles faster. Use AI to draft, then edit. Saves time on blank page syndrome.

Scheduling and Calendar Management

AI assistants can schedule meetings, manage calendars, send reminders. Saves 2-3 hours weekly.

Data Entry and CRM

AI extracts info from forms, emails, and PDFs into your CRM. Eliminates manual data entry.

Start Small

Pick one high-impact task. Automate that first. Get comfortable. Then automate the next. Don't try to automate everything at once.
