How to Update Opening Hours on Your Website Without a Developer

Keep your business hours accurate on your website. Whether it's bank holidays, seasonal schedules, or emergency closures, updating hours should take seconds — not days waiting for a developer.

Why Outdated Opening Hours Cost You Business

A customer checks your website to see if you're open. The hours say you close at 6 PM. They drive over. You're closed. They assume you've gone out of business — and never come back.

This happens more often than most business owners realize. Summer schedules change. Bank holidays come and go. You decide to extend hours on weekends. An emergency forces an unexpected closure. And each time, your website becomes a source of frustration instead of a helpful resource.

The problem is familiar to anyone who's tried updating a website: it's too slow. You have to contact your web developer, wait days for them to respond, pay a €50–€150 fee for a 30-second change, and hope they get it right. So most businesses don't bother updating their hours as frequently as they should.

According to recent surveys, up to 40% of small business websites show outdated opening hours. That's not a minor inconvenience — it's a direct loss of customer traffic and revenue.

The Problem with Current Solutions

Several existing tools try to solve this problem, but each has significant limitations:

Google Business Profile

Google Business Profile lets you set hours that appear in Google Maps and search results. It's free and relatively easy to use. But it doesn't update your website itself — customers who visit your actual site still see the old hours. And you can only set standard recurring hours, not special exceptions or temporary closures.

DIY CMS Platforms (WordPress, Wix, Squarespace)

These give you a dashboard to edit hours yourself. But you still need to log in, remember your password, navigate the interface, and find the right section. For a non-technical owner, this friction means updates get skipped. Plus, editing a CMS requires a computer — you can't do it from your phone in a natural way.

Hiring a Developer

Fast and guaranteed to be correct, but expensive (€75–€150 per hour) and slow (2–5 business days turnaround). This only makes sense if you're making dozens of changes per month.

How to Update Hours: Step by Step

1. Message Your AI Assistant

Send a WhatsApp message describing the hours change in plain English. Be as specific or casual as you like — the AI is designed to understand natural language instructions.

2. Get Confirmation

Your assistant acknowledges the request and either makes the change immediately or asks clarifying questions if needed. Most simple updates happen within minutes.

3. Verify the Change

Your site is updated and live. Your customers see the correct hours. You're done.

This is in stark contrast to the traditional approach: contact developer → wait 2–5 days → review change → request revision (if needed) → wait again → finally live.

When You Might Update Hours (And Why It Matters)

  • Seasonal changes: Summer vs. winter hours, holiday schedules
  • Special events: Extended hours for product launches, weekend markets, or festivals
  • Unexpected closures: Staff illness, emergencies, or unforeseen circumstances
  • Staffing changes: Adding or reducing hours based on available staff
  • Growing your business: Extending hours as you scale up
  • Bank holidays: Different hours or complete closure on national holidays

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I set different hours for different days of the week?+

Absolutely. You can set unique hours for every day, or specify rules like "Monday–Friday 9 AM–6 PM, weekends 10 AM–4 PM." The system is flexible enough to handle any schedule.

What if I need to close for a single day?+

Just message "Closed tomorrow for staff training" or specify the date. The system can handle temporary closures without affecting your regular schedule.

How long does it actually take to update hours?+

The request takes 30 seconds to send via WhatsApp. Implementation typically happens within 1–2 hours for most platforms. You'll receive a confirmation message once the change is live.

Will the hours show on Google Maps and other places?+

WebAssist updates your website directly. For Google Maps and other third-party platforms, you'd update those separately (though many integrations are possible). We recommend keeping Google Business Profile in sync for best results.
